# Simplicity and Professionalism Rule

1. **Simple, Standard Terminology**:
   - Always use plain, standard software engineering terminology throughout the codebase.
   - Avoid buzzwords, gimmicky naming, and unnecessary conceptual abstractions.
   - Functions, types, interfaces, and packages should clearly describe what they do.

2. **Professional Output**:
   - CLI outputs, interactive prompts, and log messages must be clean, concise, and professional.
   - Provide clear, actionable diagnostics rather than vague or cryptic errors.

3. **Codebase Maintainability**:
   - Prefer simple, readable implementations over clever or convoluted patterns.
